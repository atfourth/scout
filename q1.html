Department of Computer Science

Department

Computer science is the systematic study of computers, algorithmic processes, and computational systems to solve complex problems. It spans both theoretical foundations—such as data structures and cryptography—and practical applications like software engineering, artificial intelligence, and network security. By blending mathematics and logic, computer scientists design efficient software, analyze hardware performance, and develop cutting-edge technologies.
